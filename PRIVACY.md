# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

Everyday Oura Sync is a private personal application used only by its owner.

## Data collected

The application accesses only Oura data authorized by the user through the Oura API. The current integration uses sleep, activity, and readiness data available through the authorized `daily` scope.

## How the data is used

The data is used only for the owner's personal tracking, reporting, summaries, and training-readiness guidance.

The application may send a limited derived daily summary to Google Gemini to generate a qualitative explanation. Oura data is not sold, made public, or used for advertising or marketing.

## Storage and security

Oura data may be stored in private Google Sheets and Google Drive associated with the owner's Google account. OAuth credentials and token state are stored through Google Apps Script's properties mechanism and are not intentionally published.

## Sharing and third-party processing

The application uses Oura and Google services to operate. Data is not intentionally disclosed to other people. Google services, including Gemini where used, may process the limited data necessary to provide the application's functions.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained for the owner's personal use. The owner may revoke the application's access to Oura data at any time. If access is revoked or deletion is requested, processing will cease and stored Oura user data will be deleted within 72 hours.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/martinpola).
